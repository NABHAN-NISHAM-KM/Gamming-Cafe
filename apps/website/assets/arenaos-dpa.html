Data-Processing Agreement

Between the venue operator named below ("Controller") and ArenaOS ("Processor"), as part of the Controller's ArenaOS subscription ("Agreement").

1. Subject and duration

The Processor processes personal data only to provide ArenaOS to the Controller, for as long as the subscription runs and the period in section 9.

2. What is processed

Data subjectsThe Controller's players and customers; the Controller's staff.
Personal dataNames, usernames, phone numbers, email addresses, date of birth where the venue enables age rules, wallet and membership balances, bookings, play sessions, orders, receipts, loyalty and tournament records; staff sign-in records and audit entries.
PurposeRunning the venue: sessions, bookings, sales, wallets, loyalty, reports and support.
Special categoriesNone are required. The Controller should not enter them.

3. Processor's duties

The Processor will: process the data only on the Controller's documented instructions (using ArenaOS as configured counts as instructions); keep it confidential and bind its personnel to confidentiality; apply the security measures in section 5; help the Controller answer data-subject requests (export and erasure tools are built in); notify the Controller without undue delay, and within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a personal-data breach; and make available the information needed to show compliance.

4. Sub-processors

Sub-processorPurposeLocation
Amazon Web ServicesHosting, database, backupsSingapore
Payment provider chosen by the Controller (e.g. Stripe)Card payments, when enabledAs stated by the provider
Messaging provider chosen by the ControllerSMS / WhatsApp / email notices, when enabledAs stated by the provider

The Processor will give 30 days' notice of new sub-processors; the Controller may object on reasonable grounds.

5. Security measures

Tenant isolation enforced in the database (row-level security); passwords hashed with argon2id; optional two-step sign-in with encrypted secrets; role-based access; tamper-evident audit log for sensitive actions; TLS for all traffic; encrypted off-server backups; least-privilege access for the Processor's staff.

6. International transfers

Where data leaves the Controller's country, the parties rely on the transfer safeguards required by the applicable law (for example standard contractual clauses).

7. Audits

Once a year, or after a breach, the Controller may ask for a written report on the measures in section 5, and on reasonable notice audit them at its own cost.

8. Liability

As set out in the Agreement.

9. End of processing

After the subscription ends, the Controller may export its data for 30 days. The Processor then deletes it, except where law requires keeping it, and removes it from backups as they expire (within 90 days).

10. Governing law

The law that governs the Agreement.
