How a session flows

From walk-in to logout, without a single spreadsheet

Check in

Walk-in, booking or app. Top up the wallet or sell a time package at the desk.

Unlock

Customer signs in at the PC with password or PIN — or staff start it from the Live Floor.

Play & order

Games launch from the Shell; food ordered from the seat lands on the kitchen display.

Warn & extend

Warnings at 30/15/10/5/1 min. Extend from the seat, the desk or the app.

Settle

One combined bill. Unused prepaid time is refunded and loyalty points land instantly.

Built like infrastructure

Secure, multi-tenant and offline-ready

A hybrid cloud + branch-edge architecture keeps the floor running when the internet doesn't.

Three-layer isolation

Row-level security in the database, a tenant-scoped client and route authorization — tested in CI.

Signed commands

Per-branch keys sign every device command; PCs verify and reject replays.

Offline edge

A branch edge server keeps sessions, POS and the Shell working through outages, then syncs.

Tamper-evident audit

Append-only ledgers and a hash-chained audit log. Super Admin sign-in needs two-step verification.
