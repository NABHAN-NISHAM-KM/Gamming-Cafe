How a session flows

From walk-in to logout, without a single spreadsheet

Check in

Walk-in, booking or app. Top up the wallet or sell a time package at the desk.

Unlock

Customer signs in at the PC with password or PIN — or staff start it from the Live Floor.

Play & order

Games launch from the Shell; food ordered from the seat lands on the kitchen display.

Warn & extend

Warnings at 30/15/10/5/1 min. Extend from the seat, the desk or the app.

Settle

One combined bill. Unused prepaid time is refunded and loyalty points land instantly.
