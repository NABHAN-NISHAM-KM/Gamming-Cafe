FAQ

Questions, answered

What counts as a station?

Any device you bill time on: a gaming PC running the ArenaOS agent, a console, a VR rig, a racing simulator or an internet-café PC. Staff PCs, POS terminals and kitchen displays don't count.

What happens when the internet goes down?

The Windows agent keeps a local fail-safe lock and the session state, so players are never locked out mid-game and never get free time. The branch edge server (Offline Edge) keeps sessions and the POS running and syncs everything back when the connection returns.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing period as long as you're within the lower plan's limits.

Do you support my hardware and launchers?

The agent runs on Windows 10/11 PCs. The game library supports the major launchers (Steam, Epic, Riot, Battle.net, EA, Ubisoft) and detects installs automatically. Consoles, VR and TVs run agentless.

Which payment methods and currencies?

Your venue can take cash, card and mixed payments through the POS, with wallets in your local currency. ArenaOS supports AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, BHD, OMR, INR, GBP and USD out of the box.

Is my data isolated from other venues?

Yes — three independent layers: PostgreSQL row-level security, a tenant-scoped data client, and route-level authorization. Each layer is tested in CI on every change.
