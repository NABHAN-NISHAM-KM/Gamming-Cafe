Stations & Live Floor

A .NET 8 Windows agent on every PC talks to the cloud (or your branch edge) and paints a real-time map of the whole venue.

Live Floor map

  • Status within 1 s: free, in use, reserved, ending, offline, maintenance
  • Drag-and-drop layout editor per zone
  • Station drawer with customer, game, time, hardware

Remote control

  • Lock, unlock, restart, shutdown, message, Wake-on-LAN
  • Mass actions for an entire zone
  • Commands queued for offline PCs and signed per branch

Monitoring & alerts

  • WMI hardware inventory and NVIDIA GPU telemetry
  • Alerts for temperature, low disk, hardware changes, offline
  • Enrolment by code, device key protected by DPAPI

Sessions, pricing & the Gaming Shell

Server-authoritative sessions that end on time — even if the PC, the agent, the API or Redis restarts.

Pricing engine

  • Rates by zone, branch or organization
  • Time windows in the branch's timezone (happy hours)
  • Tiers, packages, passes, postpaid, per-minute
  • Membership and staff discounts; quotes snapshotted per session

Session control

  • Start, extend, move and end — each one transactional and idempotent
  • Warnings at 30 / 15 / 10 / 5 / 1 minutes
  • Unused prepaid time refunded on logout
  • Customer sign-in at the PC with password or PIN

Gaming Shell

  • WPF + WebView2 host with a hardened named pipe
  • Lock screen, countdown ring, EN/AR
  • Local fail-safe lock when the network is down
  • In-seat food ordering and staff messages

Games & station tools

Keep hundreds of PCs on the same, up-to-date library without walking the floor with a USB stick.

Signed game library

  • Steam, Epic, Riot, Battle.net and more launchers
  • Automatic install detection per PC
  • Central update orchestration by zone

Peripherals & connectivity

  • Peripheral profiles and accessory tracking
  • Ping and connectivity check from the seat
  • Headset, mouse and display fixes players can run themselves

Repairs & diskless

  • Remote repair scripts from the Live Floor
  • Maintenance sessions for technicians
  • Adapter layer for diskless boot systems

Consoles, VR, simulators & printing

Agentless stations are first-class citizens: same timers, same bills, same Live Floor.

Console & VR stations

  • Player-based pricing (included players + extra player rate)
  • Cleaning-required flag between VR sessions
  • Racing sims with short packages

TV station displays

  • Pair a TV with a code, show its timer and status
  • Smart-plug bridge to power screens on/off with sessions

Internet café printing

  • Print jobs held for approval and charged per page
  • Per-minute browsing and office-app plans

Customers, wallet & bookings

One account across every branch — with a wallet ledger your accountant will love.

Wallet

  • Cash, bonus, promo and gift buckets plus prepaid time
  • Append-only ledger, idempotent top-ups
  • Balances can never go negative — enforced by the database

Memberships

  • Tiers with discounts and point multipliers
  • Restrictions (age, bans) enforced at sign-in

Bookings & customer app

  • Book PCs, consoles, VR or whole zones
  • No double booking — enforced with a database exclusion constraint
  • Mobile PWA: wallet, bookings, shop, rewards, tournaments, inbox

POS, restaurant & kitchen

Gaming time, snacks and a full restaurant menu on one combined bill.

Unified POS

  • Sell time, products, packages and top-ups
  • Split and mixed payments, refunds with reasons
  • Cash drawers, shifts and cash movements

Restaurant & tables

  • Table map, dine-in, takeaway and in-seat orders
  • Modifiers and course firing

Kitchen display (KDS)

  • Tickets by station with timers
  • Bump, recall and ready notifications to the seat

Inventory & purchasing

Know exactly how many energy drinks you have — per branch, per warehouse, per lot.

Stock ledger

  • Lots, movements, transfers between branches
  • Stock counts and variance
  • Waste tracking with reasons

Recipes

  • Planned waste % per ingredient

Purchasing

  • Suppliers, purchase orders and receiving
  • Supplier invoices matched to receipts

Loyalty, promotions, tournaments & CRM

The growth engine: reward regulars, fill quiet hours and run events that pack the house.

Loyalty

  • Points for gaming, food, minutes played, bookings kept, tournaments, referrals, birthdays
  • Tier multipliers, FIFO expiry, refunds reverse points
  • Rewards: free minutes, wallet credit, one-use discount or product codes

Promotions engine

  • Deterministic rules: conditions → effects, best non-stackable plus stackables, capped
  • Happy hours, weekend bonus minutes, birthday and first-visit offers
  • Shared and personal codes with budgets and per-customer limits

Tournaments

  • Single/double elimination, round robin, league and Swiss
  • Seeding with byes, score reporting, automatic advancement
  • Entry fees via POS or wallet; wallet prizes and points

CRM & campaigns

  • Built-in and custom segments, static lists
  • Consent-only campaigns: in-app inbox, Shell message, SMS, email, WhatsApp
  • Personal promo codes per recipient

Platform, roles & security

Multi-tenant SaaS from day one, with a Super Admin console for the platform operator.

Super Admin

  • Create, suspend and activate organizations
  • Plans, limits and 26 feature flags per plan or org
  • Client releases, countries, currencies, taxes, gateways
  • Audited impersonation for support

Organizations & RBAC

  • Organization → brand → branch → zone hierarchy
  • 111 permissions, 14 role templates, custom roles
  • Anti-escalation on role grants; roles per branch

Security

  • EdDSA JWTs, refresh rotation with reuse detection, TOTP MFA
  • Row-level security in PostgreSQL; hash-chained audit log
  • Sensitive actions require a reason
