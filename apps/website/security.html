Security & privacy

Your venue's data stays yours.

Your players' wallets, phone numbers and play history are trusted to you. Here's how ArenaOS protects them, and what we sign with you.

Each venue walled off

Every row of data carries its venue, and the database itself refuses to return another venue's rows (PostgreSQL row-level security) — not just the app code.

Passwords done properly

Passwords and PINs are stored as argon2id hashes with OWASP's parameters. Sign-in timing doesn't reveal which emails exist, and repeated failures are slowed down.

Two-step sign-in

Staff and owners can turn on authenticator-app codes. Their secret is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.

Who did what

Refunds, discounts, wallet changes and other sensitive actions need a reason and land in an audit log that can't be edited. Money is kept in a double-entry ledger.

Roles, not shared logins

Each staff member signs in as themselves with only the permissions their role needs — cashier, manager, kitchen, owner, or your own roles.

Encrypted in transit

Every connection — stations, the customer app, the admin console — uses HTTPS (TLS). Stations work offline and sync once the line is back.

Players' rights

Venues can export a player's data or erase it on request; erasure removes personal details and keeps only the anonymous money records the law requires.

Backups

The database is backed up before every change we release and on a schedule, kept encrypted and off the server.

Hosting

ArenaOS runs on Amazon Web Services in Singapore (ap-southeast-1). Ask us if your country needs data kept in-region.

Our own website

This site counts page visits without cookies, IP addresses or browser fingerprints, and skips counting when your browser asks not to be tracked.

Data-processing agreement

You're the controller of your players' data; ArenaOS processes it for you. Our DPA sets that out — what we process, why, where, for how long, and how we help you answer requests.
