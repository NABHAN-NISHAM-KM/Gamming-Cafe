Switching café software without closing: an opening-week checklist

3 October 2026

Changing the software that runs your venue feels risky: the PCs, the till and every player's balance depend on it. Done in the right order, you can switch without closing for a single day.

1. Write down what you have

Before you touch anything, list:

  • every PC, console and VR station, and which area it's in;
  • your rates, packages, happy hours and membership levels;
  • your staff and what each one is allowed to do (refunds, discounts, opening the till);
  • your menu, if you sell food and drinks.

This list becomes your setup — and you'll be surprised what's been forgotten.

2. Set up prices and staff first

Enter areas, rates and packages, then staff accounts with their own logins and roles. No more shared passwords at the counter — every refund and discount should show who did it.

3. Pilot one area

Install the new station software on a few PCs in one area, for example the VIP room. Run them for a couple of days alongside the old system. Staff learn on a small area, and if something's wrong, only a few PCs are affected.

4. Move the players' money carefully

Players' prepaid balances and memberships are the most sensitive part. Agree a cut-over moment (a quiet morning), note every balance in the old system, and add each one to the new system with a clear note. Ask regulars to check their balance at their next visit.

5. Roll out the rest

Once the pilot runs cleanly, install the remaining PCs area by area. Each PC enrols with your branch's code, and is on the floor plan from then on.

6. Tell your players

Put a QR poster at the counter and on the tables so players get the app: they can see free PCs, book and top up without waiting at the counter. Publish your venue page so new players can find you.

7. Close the first week's books

At the end of each shift in the first week, count the till and compare it with the system's figures. Small differences early are usually training; catch them while the week is still fresh.
